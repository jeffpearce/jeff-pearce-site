**I’m Jeff Pearce, a macOS and iOS developer focused on building clear, reliable native tools for Apple platforms.**

I’ve spent decades in the software industry working across Windows, macOS, iOS, and cloud services — from desktop operating systems to Dockerized AWS systems. That breadth has shaped how I think about tools: they should be fast, predictable, and feel like they truly belong on the platform they’re built for.

My passion has long been developer tools. My first industry work involved leading development of build tracking and release software on mainframes at GM/EDS, back when such tools didn’t yet exist. Later, as an SDET at Microsoft, I spent my off-hours adding networking support to the internal debugging app so developers could diagnose issues remotely from the comfort of their offices for the first time. It became surprisingly popular.

Over the years I’ve worked on the Windows OS, contributed to service infrastructure for Zune and Xbox, built cloud‑based systems using Docker and AWS, and even worked on Windows Phone (RIP). But my passion for the last 20 years has been macOS and iOS, where I found the kind of craftsmanship and predictability I wanted as both a developer and a musician. I’ve created native macOS tools for documentation, writing, and development workflows, including Help Crafter and its successor, Help Architect. I’ve also built a native iOS ham radio logging app designed for field use, with cloud sync and integrations with popular lookup services.

I’ve built internal tools, prototypes, and even a native diff/merge app that may still see daylight someday.

I care deeply about software that feels at home on Apple platforms — apps that respect the conventions, typography, and interaction patterns that make macOS and iOS unique. Whether I’m building tools for developers, writers, or niche communities, my goal is always the same: **make focused, reliable software that helps people work more effectively.**

Outside of software, I produce electronic ambient music — a creative outlet I’ve kept for years. It’s something I do for the joy of it, usually on quiet weekends or late nights. If you're curious, you can find some of my work on [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/5ikDYEPeTHfts5TyapV40J), [Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/artist/audiosinner/1797614753), or [Soundcloud](https://soundcloud.com/audiosinner-1)

I’m also a licensed Amateur Extra radio operator — K7MG — active in CW and [Parks On The Air](https://parksontheair.com). I enjoy portable operating around the Pacific Northwest, chasing contacts from trailheads, beaches, and forest overlooks. If you hear me calling CQ, I’d love to QSO.
